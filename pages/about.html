AI security researcher from Australia. I build adversarial evaluation infrastructure for autonomous AI agents.

My Master's capstone at the University of Sydney is a real-world evaluation framework for autonomous pentesting LLMs — comparing four fine-tuned Qwen3-14B variants across 32 controlled cells on Hack The Box machines, with behaviour mapped to OWASP Agentic Top 10, NIST AI 600-1, MITRE ATLAS, and the Singapore Agentic AI Framework. The framework is published open-source as beyond-ctfs.

Methodological integrity is the point — null-tolerant reporting, pre-registered hypotheses, blind scoring, honest inter-rater reliability disclosure. Not benchmark theatre.

Background

Came up through a few desk jobs before committing to cybersecurity full-time. The goal is to contribute to Australia's cybersecurity space in a meaningful way — always looking for opportunities where I can keep learning and actually make an impact.
