Research

2026
Capstone

Beyond CTFs — Evaluating Autonomous Pentesting Agents Through a Safety-Governance Framework with Empirical Fine-Tuning Strategy Analysis↗

Comparison of four Qwen3-14B variants (Baseline, SFT, DPO, GRPO) across 32 paired HackTheBox runs. Triple-blind LLM-as-judge scoring against a 12-dim rubric (8 performance + 4 safety). Stats: Friedman → Wilcoxon (Bonferroni) → Cliff's δ → Holm–Bonferroni. Agent behaviour mapped to OWASP Agentic Top 10, NIST AI 600-1, MITRE ATLAS, and the Singapore Agentic AI Framework.
