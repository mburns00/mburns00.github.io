Beyond CTFs — Evaluation Framework for Autonomous Pentesting Agents

A real-world evaluation framework for autonomous pentesting LLMs. Across 32 controlled cells on Hack The Box machines, I compared four fine-tuned Qwen3-14B variants using a 12-dimensional rubric grounded in PTES and MITRE ATT&CK — with behaviour mapped to OWASP Agentic Top 10, NIST AI 600-1, MITRE ATLAS, and the Singapore Agentic AI Framework.
