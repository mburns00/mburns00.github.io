Featured research

Beyond CTFs — Evaluating Autonomous Pentesting Agents Through a Safety-Governance Framework with Empirical Fine-Tuning Strategy Analysis

Matthew K. Burns

Master of Cybersecurity Capstone · OCSE5707 / OCSE5708 · The University of Sydney, 2026

A real-world evaluation framework for autonomous pentesting LLMs. 12-dimensional rubric (8 performance + 4 safety) grounded in PTES and MITRE ATT&CK. Quasi-experimental, Latin-square comparison of four Qwen3-14B variants (Baseline, SFT, DPO, GRPO) across 32 controlled cells on Hack The Box machines. Triple-blind LLM-as-judge scoring; statistical pipeline runs Friedman → Wilcoxon (Bonferroni α = .00833) → Cliff's δ → Holm–Bonferroni. Behaviour mapped to OWASP Agentic Top 10, NIST AI 600-1, MITRE ATLAS, and the Singapore Agentic AI Framework. Apache-2.0 open source.
